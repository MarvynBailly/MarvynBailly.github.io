PewPew

An Asteroids-Inspired Arcade Game

PewPew is a fast-paced arcade shooter inspired by the classic game Asteroids. Built with p5.js, it features smooth controls, power-ups, and custom pixel art sprites created by my friend Uriah Barzola.

Gameplay

The objective is simple: survive as long as possible while destroying enemies. But don't let the simple premise fool you—things get hectic quickly!

Controls

  • WASD - Move your ship
  • Mouse Click - Shoot bullets

Power-Ups

Throughout the game, power-ups spawn that enhance your abilities. Collect them to gain advantages like increased fire rate, spread shots, or temporary invincibility.

Development

PewPew was built using p5.js, a JavaScript library that makes creative coding accessible. The game features:

  • Smooth 60 FPS gameplay
  • Collision detection between bullets, enemies, and the player
  • Particle effects for explosions and impacts
  • Progressive difficulty scaling

Art & Design

All sprites and visual assets were created by Uriah Barzola, giving the game its distinctive retro-futuristic aesthetic. The pixel art style pays homage to classic arcade games while feeling fresh and modern.
