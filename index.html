About This Simulation

The burning MB is a live solution of the Navier-Stokes equations for incompressible flow, running entirely on your GPU with WebGL. The flames are heat carried and lifted by the air around the letters; the lake below is a separate solver for the wave equation, and everything is drawn as ASCII as it is computed.

How to Interact

  • Move the mouse: Blow wind through the flames and the trees
  • Click and hold: Set the air on fire; drag to draw with flame
  • Over the water: Trail ripples across the lake
  • Hold fire at the trees: They catch, burn down, and grow back in time
  • Settings: Switch the ASCII off, change its colours, or try other scenes

Techniques Used

  • Semi-Lagrangian advection for unconditional stability
  • Pressure projection via Jacobi iteration
  • Vorticity confinement to keep the flames turbulent
  • Buoyancy, so heat rises and the fire draws its own shape
  • A leapfrog wave-equation solver for the lake, with reflections bent by its slope
  • A GPU ASCII renderer that picks each glyph from the flow, edges and all

Acknowledgments
